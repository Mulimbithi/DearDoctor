Questions you may have:

Is DearDoctor free to use?

Yes, DearDoctor is absolutely free to use. We strive to provide all standard features for free to ensure that financial issues do not come between you and the care you need.

How can I trust the consultant assigned to me?

All our consultants are qualified and assesed before being onboarded to the program. Their credentials are verified by both the Ministry of Health and the Counsellors and Psychologists board so you are guaranteed to receive quality help!

What happens if I need immediate help?

DearDoctor is not a crisis intervention service. For immediate help, please call 116 or your local helpline.Docbot also provides suggestions for youth friendly institutions you can visit.For ongoing support, you can reach out to your consultant once they start the communication process.

How do I opt out of the service?

You can opt out of the service at any time by deleting your account.All your information will be permanently cleared from our database.

How do I know my information is safe?

DearDoctor takes your privacy seriously. All your information is encrypted and stored securely. We adhere to strict data protection policies to ensure that your personal details remain confidential.
